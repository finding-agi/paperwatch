Reset frequency and haircut come from a 14-day backtest on 5m candles (ETH/BTC), interpolated between tested reset sizes. Past volatility is not a forecast, and the backtest does not model slow closes, so real cost per PAPER runs a little higher. The bot stops reopening once free balance can't cover the next pair's margin + $1. PAPER can't be transferred and has no market price. Not financial advice.
