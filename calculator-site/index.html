Unofficial community tool, not affiliated with Papertrade. Reset frequency and haircut come from a 14-day backtest on 5m candles, interpolated between tested reset sizes; past volatility is not a forecast and slow closes aren't modeled, so real cost per PAPER runs a little higher. A bot stops reopening once free balance can't cover the next pair's margin + $1. Not financial advice.
